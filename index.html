Technology

Three signals.
One life protected.

DrowseGuard runs on a compact hardware unit using MediaPipe's 478-point face mesh to track three independent drowsiness signals simultaneously — no cloud, no latency.

Detection 01

Eye Aspect Ratio

Tracks the ratio of eyelid height to width across 6 facial landmarks per eye. When eyes begin closing, EAR drops below threshold — persistent low EAR across consecutive frames triggers a drowsiness alert.

ThresholdEAR < 0.25
Trigger frames20 consecutive
Response time~2.0 seconds
Detection 02

Mouth Aspect Ratio

Measures mouth opening using 4 landmark points. A wide, sustained opening indicates yawning — one of the earliest and most reliable physiological precursors to microsleep onset in drivers.

ThresholdMAR > 0.60
Trigger frames15 consecutive
Response time~1.5 seconds
Detection 03

Head Nod Detection

Tracks nose tip Y-position against a self-calibrating baseline. Forward head drop signals microsleep onset. Baseline auto-calibrates in 20 frames — works instantly for every driver, including those with turbans or beards.

ThresholdDrop > 0.08
Trigger frames12 consecutive
Response time~1.2 seconds
